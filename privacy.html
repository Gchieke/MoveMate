Privacy Policy — MoveMate

Last updated: October 9, 2026

MoveMate ("we", "our") is a neighborhood app that lets residents post move-in/move-out requests and lets neighbors volunteer to help. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used for sign-in and account security.
  • Profile data: display name and the neighborhood you join.
  • Move requests: the details you post (date, area, description, help needed). Exact addresses are visible only to members of your neighborhood.
  • Contact preferences: if you opt in, a phone number for SMS alerts about moves in your neighborhood.

How we use your data

  • To operate the app: show move requests to your neighborhood and coordinate volunteers.
  • To send you alerts you asked for: email and (if opted in) SMS notifications about new moves in your neighborhood.
  • To keep accounts secure (authentication, password resets).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show third-party advertising.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of app data.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (move alerts, sign-in emails).
  • Twilio — SMS delivery, only if you opt in to text alerts.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your move requests in the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, email privacy@movemateapp.co from your account email address and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

MoveMate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will note the new "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@movemateapp.co.
